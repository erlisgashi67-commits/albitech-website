In-house technology

Albismart: the automation behind every unit

Albismart is our proprietary platform connecting intake, testing, repair, grading, provisioning and dispatch. It removes manual steps, enforces the same quality standard on every device and gives you full traceability by serial number.

  • Automated diagnostics and test routines
  • Serial-level tracking from intake to dispatch
  • Live order status and reporting
  • Rules-based grading for consistent quality
