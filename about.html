About Albitech

Refurbishment built on partnership

We help businesses get more value, more life and more reliability from every device they own, sell or support.

Our story

A device that can be repaired should not be replaced

AlbiTech L.L.C. was founded by Kujtim Gashi on 13 November 2018, on a simple conviction: well-made electronics deserve more than one life. From our base in Kijevë, Malishevë, on the Pejë-Prishtinë highway, we built a team of skilled technicians and our own warehouse and logistics operation, so devices move from intake to dispatch without leaving our hands.

Today we refurbish, remanufacture, test, repair and recycle IT and telecommunications hardware at scale for telecom operators, retailers and enterprises. Our own Albismart platform runs it all, with industrial management, real-time tracking and AI-driven performance data.

Our mission

Success starts when we grow together

Our growth is measured by our clients' growth. We take on their device challenges as our own, share data openly and build long-term partnerships rather than one-off jobs.

Our team

Good people do good work

Our strongest asset is the team on the floor: hard-working, well trained and proud of what leaves the building.

Women hold many of our technical and quality roles, from testing and repair to final inspection and packing. We invest in training so every person can grow into more responsibility, and we keep the same standard on every shift.

  • Trained on documented procedures for each device family
  • Women in technical and quality roles across the line
  • A careful, reliable team, shift after shift

Sustainability

Refurbishment is the greener choice

Every device we repair or refurbish is one less device in the waste stream, and one less new device that has to be manufactured.

  • Repair and refurbishment extend the useful life of devices
  • Working parts are recovered and reused where possible
  • Secure data erasure makes safe reuse and resale possible
  • Devices beyond repair are recycled responsibly, as part of our own service
