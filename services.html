Our services

Every stage of the device lifecycle

Five integrated services, one controlled process. Use them individually or combine them into a single programme managed end to end by Albitech.

  • Repair
  • Refurbishment
  • Quality control
  • Inventory & logistics
  • Provisioning
01

Fast, reliable repair services

Our technicians diagnose faults down to component level and repair them with quality parts, following documented procedures for each device family. Every repair is tested before it leaves the bench, so devices come back working, not just patched.

Ideal for

  • Warranty repairs
  • Out-of-warranty service
  • Swap-pool replenishment

Benefits

  • Short, predictable turnaround times
  • Component-level diagnostics and repair
  • Full repair history for every serial number
  • Functional testing after every repair
02

Advanced device refurbishment

We refurbish and remanufacture used and returned IT and telecom devices to sale-ready condition through cleaning, cosmetic restoration, part replacement and secure data erasure. What cannot be saved is recycled responsibly. Each unit is graded against clear criteria, so you know exactly what you are selling or redeploying.

Ideal for

  • Trade-in programmes
  • Customer returns
  • Remarketing

Benefits

  • Secure data erasure with a report per device
  • Consistent, documented cosmetic grading
  • More value recovered from returns and trade-ins
  • Longer device life and less e-waste
03

Automated quality control with Albismart

Albismart runs standardised test routines on every device and records each result automatically. Human judgement is reserved for the decisions that need it, which removes variation between operators and shifts.

Ideal for

  • High-volume programmes
  • Strict quality targets
  • Audited supply chains

Benefits

  • The same test standard on every unit
  • Automated grading and pass or fail decisions
  • Test reports available for each device
  • Fewer returns from end customers
04

Inventory & logistics automation by Albismart

From inbound pallets to outbound parcels, Albismart tracks stock by serial number and location. Orders are picked, packed and dispatched from our own warehouse, with live status visible to your team at every step.

Ideal for

  • Stock buffering
  • Order fulfilment
  • Reverse logistics

Benefits

  • Real-time visibility of stock and orders
  • Serialised tracking by location
  • Automated picking and dispatch flows
  • Lower handling costs and fewer errors
05

Device provisioning & pre-configuration systems

We configure devices to your specification before they ship: software images, settings, accounts, enrolment and labelling. Your users unbox a device that is ready to work, and your IT team skips the set-up queue.

Ideal for

  • Enterprise roll-outs
  • Retail store devices
  • Telecom customer equipment

Benefits

  • Ready-to-use devices on day one
  • Consistent configuration across whole fleets
  • Asset tagging and custom labelling
  • Less IT workload during roll-outs
