Warehouse & facility

A modern tech facility

A modern tech facility for storage, logistics and operational support. Our warehouse in Kijevë brings storage, repair lines, testing, provisioning and dispatch under one roof, connected by Albismart.

What happens here

Storage, logistics and operational support

Keeping every stage in one controlled building cuts transport between sites, shortens lead times and keeps every device within our chain of custody.

Secure storage

Organised pallet racking with controlled access and location-level stock records.

Inbound & outbound

Receiving, consolidation, picking, packing and dispatch handled by our own team.

Operational support

Dedicated repair, test and provisioning areas next to storage, so devices never wait for transport.

Tracked handling

Every pallet, box and device is scanned in Albismart at each move.
